# Nature Video

Place your Bangladesh nature video file here as:

    bangladesh-nature.mp4

Recommended: 1920x1080 or larger, under ~15MB, muted/loopable footage (rivers, tea
gardens, hills, or drone shots of Bangladesh). Until a video file is added, the
Home page will simply show the poster image (`/images/hero/bg-image.jpg`) in that
section instead of playing a video — nothing will break.
